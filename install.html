Install the Mold Job App

About 2 minutes. No app store, no account, no password.

Scan with your tablet or phone camera, or type this address:
caselens.github.io/mold-job-app
Already on your tablet? Open the app

Android tablet or phone

  1. Open the address in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install if a pop-up offers it. If not, tap the three dots at the top right, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Mold Jobs from the new icon.
  4. Fill in the setup screen with your company details and tap Start using the app.

iPad or iPhone

  1. Open the address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Mold Jobs from the new icon and fill in the setup screen.
On iPad and iPhone, always use the home screen icon. Jobs typed in a Safari tab are kept separately and can be cleared.

Keep your jobs safe

  1. Your jobs are saved on your device only. Nobody else can see them.
  2. Once a week: Settings, then Back up now. Keep the file somewhere safe, like your email.
  3. New device? Install the app there, then Settings, then Restore a backup.
