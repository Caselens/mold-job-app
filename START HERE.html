Mold Job App

Your customers, screening, quotes, agreements, work days and payments, one page at a time.

Open the app

First time: set up your company

  1. Tap Open the app above.
  2. Type in your company name, your name, phone, email, address, MHIC license number and inspection fee.
  3. Tap Start using the app. That's it. Every quote, agreement and invoice now has your company on it.

On a laptop (Windows)

  1. Keep this folder somewhere you won't delete it, like Documents. Don't run the app from inside the zip file.
  2. Double-click START HERE, then Open the app. It opens in your web browser (Chrome or Edge).
  3. Press Ctrl + D to bookmark it, so you can open it again in one click.
  4. Always open it in the same browser. Your jobs are saved in that browser on this laptop.

On a tablet

A tablet can't open the app from a zip file. It needs the app on a web address first. Ross is setting that up.

  1. Open the web address Ross gives you, in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPad).
  2. Add it to the home screen. iPad: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open it from the new icon. After the first time, it works without internet.

Keep your jobs safe

Jobs are saved on the device you enter them on. A laptop and a tablet don't share jobs by themselves.
  1. Back up once a week: in the app, tap Settings, then Back up now. A backup file goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. Moving to another device: make a backup, send the file to the other device (email it to yourself), then open the app there and tap Settings, then Restore a backup.
  3. Don't use a private or incognito browser window. It forgets everything when you close it.
  4. Clearing your browser's history and site data erases the jobs too. Back up first.
